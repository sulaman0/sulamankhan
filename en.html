The guest types, instead of clicking

No app, no account, no menu to scroll through. The agent understands "a large pepperoni, hold the onion" and finds the item on the menu itself.

It answers what the phone would otherwise have to

Allergens, halal, opening hours, how long the wait is. These are the questions that make people ring during the rush, or give up and order somewhere else.

It upsells without pushing

One suggestion, once, based on what is already in the basket. Average order value goes up without the guest feeling sold to.

Payment inside the conversation

Local payment methods, whatever the market actually uses: MobilePay, Apple Pay, Google Pay, cards. No redirect to a platform, and the money goes straight to the restaurant.

No commission, and the customer belongs to the restaurant

The phone number, the order and the history sit with the restaurant, not with the delivery platform. Next time they can reach the guest directly.

One agent, several channels. The same conversation runs on WhatsApp, on the restaurant's own site, on SMS and on Messenger. In most of Europe, Latin America, South Asia and the Middle East, WhatsApp is where guests already are, so that is usually the primary channel. In the Nordics it is web chat and SMS. The agent is the same either way, and the channel is chosen per market rather than assumed.

What I build

Three things, available separately. I always start with a free demo built on the restaurant's own menu, so there is something real to judge before any money changes hands.

Own ordering channel

On the restaurant's own domain. No commission per order.

  • Local payment methods and cards
  • Message to the guest when the food is ready
  • Ticket to the kitchen display or printer
  • Multiple locations on one page

AI chat agent

Takes orders and table bookings, and answers questions.

  • Multilingual, including Danish, English, German, Spanish, Urdu
  • Knows the menu, prices and allergens
  • Escalates to a human only when it needs to
  • WhatsApp, web chat, SMS, Messenger

Demand forecasting

How many guests are coming tomorrow, and what to prep.

  • Trained on the restaurant's own history
  • Day of week, season, holidays, weather, local events
  • Per daypart and per location
  • Can be run on last year's data with nothing installed
